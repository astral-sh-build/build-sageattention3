SageAttention3 builds clone CUTLASS during `sageattention3_blackwell/setup.py`
when `sageattention3_blackwell/csrc/cutlass` is absent. The resulting checkout
provides CUTLASS headers used to compile the SageAttention3 CUDA extensions.

Project: NVIDIA CUTLASS
Repository: https://github.com/NVIDIA/cutlass
Build-time path: sageattention3_blackwell/csrc/cutlass
